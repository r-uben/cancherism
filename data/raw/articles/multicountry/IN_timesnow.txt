No Yellow For Alexis Mac Allister! FIFA World Cup 2026 Final Refereeing Sparks Fresh Argentina Bias Debate
Written by
:
Shaunak Ghosh
Updated Jul 20, 2026, 01:46 IST
Spain is facing Argentina in a tense FIFA World Cup 2026 final, marred by prior controversial refereeing decisions, especially during Argentina's tournament journey. In a pivotal moment, Alexis Mac Allister's rough tackle on Dani Olmo went unpunished.
Alexi Mac Allister's challenge on Dani Olmo in the FIFA World Cup 2026 final was not even given a yellow card.

Spain are currently taking on Argentina in a highly charged up final of the FIFA World Cup 2026. Coming into this game, the tournament, especially Argentina''s run to the final has been riddled with controversy, especially some questionable calls by the referees in their games in particular. In the summit clash at the New York New Jersey Stadium, the refereeing has come into question once again, as an incident on the ground involving Alexis Mac Allister and Dani Olmo
Mac Allister's Rough Challenge On Olmo Gets No Card
During the frantic and highly competitive opening phase of the
FIFA World Cup 2026 Final
, Mac Allister committed a late, lunging tackle on Olmo in the 15th minute. Despite the apparent severity of the challenge and the immediate reaction from players on the pitch, the match official opted for a surprisingly lenient approach.
The referee firmly declined to issue a yellow card to the Argentine midfielder, opting instead for a strict verbal warning. Mac Allister, who had already committed prior fouls to disrupt the Spanish rhythm, managed to completely escape entering the referee's book.
However, while this challenge was completely ignored by the referee, a foul decision on Spain later in the game raised further questions. When Spain's Alex Baena made what appeared to be a soft, routine challenge involving a slight push on Lionel Messi, the referee immediately blew his whistle to award Argentina a foul.
Reigniting The Bias Debate
The notable lack of a booking has reignited debates regarding officiating biases that have trailed Argentina throughout this FIFA World Cup 2026.
The scrutiny began to intensify during the Round of 16 match of Argentina against Egypt. As the African nation desperately pushed for a late equalizer in a narrow 3-2 defeat, the match official handed out an unprecedented five separate yellow cards to Egyptian players entirely within stoppage time (between the 90th and 103rd minutes), effectively killing their late momentum.
In fact, one of their goals were cancelled out because of a challenge that happened on the opposite side of the pitch before the build up to the goal began. Also, a late foul on Mohamed Salah, which directly led to Argentina's third goal was also not given. In fact, Egypt's coach and players called the officiating out as biased and rigged in favour of Argentina.
This narrative gained even more traction in the quarter-final clash with Switzerland. With the match delicately poised, a controversial second yellow card was shown to a Swiss player in the 72nd minute. The subsequent dismissal reduced Switzerland to ten men, directly allowing Argentina to dominate the remainder of the match and ultimately secure a 3-1 victory in extra time.
