MICAH RICHARDS believed the controversial decision not to book Alexis Mac Allister’s early challenge in the World Cup final “sets a dangerous precedent”.
Sunday night’s showpiece started off in hectic fashion with a number of early chances and tackles flying in.

Former Three Lions
striker
Alan Shearer
agreed and called Mac Allister “very fortunate” to escape the booking, while Roy Keane said he “got away with one there”.
Chris Sutton
was also critical of the decision and said it summed up Argentina’s status as “a bunch of bar room brawlers”.
He said: “It’s a late challenge. That has to be a yellow card, and guess what? The referee hasn’t gone to his pocket.
“This is what Argentina do and what they are good at. They are a bunch of bar room brawlers.

“And if they get away with it they will. Get a grip referee.”
England’s former No1 keeper
Joe Hart
weighed in too, calling it “a big decision” from the referee that “gives Argentina a green light to do whatever they want.”
Fans were baffled too as one wrote on X: “How is this not even a yellow card for Mac Allister???”
Another said: “That’s a straight up yellow card. Mac Allister what were you thinking?”
Advertisement
A third added: “That’s a yellow card in most games. Lucky escape.”
Eventually Argentina’s Lisandro Martinez became the first player to be booked in the final after hacking down Mikel Oyarzabal from behind.
But that didn’t come until the 41st minute and came after
Cristian Romero
also was fortunate not to be booked.
However Argentina were ultimately undone by their ill-discipline when Enzo Fernadandez saw red for two quick bookings.
That put the South Americans down to 10-men before Ferran Torres found an extra-time winner as
Spain won the World Cup
.
