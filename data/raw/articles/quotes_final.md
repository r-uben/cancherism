## F01 — Mac Allister on Olmo (~14–15′)

**BBC (Phil McNulty report / live page)**  
> Spain constantly complained about referee Slavko Vincic's leniency, especially when Alexis Mac Allister was late on Dani Olmo, with the first yellow card coming after 40 minutes when Lisandro Martinez brought down Oyarzabal…

**Guardian (Jacob Steinberg analysis)**  
> Alexis Mac Allister could have been booked for an early foul on Dani Olmo and the leniency, together with a pitch not conducive to playing flowing football, allowed Argentina to disrupt Spain’s passing game…

**The Athletic**  
> Alexis Mac Allister’s early foul on Dani Olmo was badly timed and nasty. … Both walked away without punishment.

**Mirror, quoting ex-ref Graham Scott (via Athletic interview)**  
> The Liverpool midfielder’s tackle on the halfway line early in the first half was late, involved both feet and could only be described as reckless. … Mac Allister crossed the line in the 15th minute and should have been punished.

**Liverpool.com / Gary Neville (ITV half-time)**
