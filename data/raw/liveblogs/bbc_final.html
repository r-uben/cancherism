  10. Emotional Scaloni leaves news conference in tearspublished at 00:42 BST 20 July
    00:42 BST 20 July

    FT: Spain 1-0 Argentina

    Neil Johnston
    BBC Sport at New York New Jersey Stadium

    I'm in the media centre at the New York New Jersey Stadium where Argentina boss Lionel Scaloni has just had to cut-short his news conference after breaking down in tears.

    Scaloni looked distraught as he answered a question about whether this was Lionel Messi's last game.

    He said he did not talk to Messi about it and then went on to say he will reflect on Argentina's performance at this World Cup and talk to the president.

    "It is very painful, I am very sorry," said an emotional Scaloni before leaving the room.
