First ever World Cup Halftime show

True to Super Bowl tradition, the FIFA World Cup will feature its first halftime show. What better place to hold it than a city that produces the best shows year after year?

For the first time in World Cup history, fans will enjoy a full-scale halftime show during the final. The 17-minute spectacular (11 minutes of performances plus 6 minutes for stage setup) promises to be unforgettable at MetLife Stadium.

The star-studded lineup includes Justin Bieber, Madonna, Shakira, Burna Boy, Coldplay, Gustavo Dudamel conducting, BTS, and the PS22 Chorus. This marks a major evolution for the tournament, bringing Super Bowl-level entertainment to football's biggest stage.

It's a fitting celebration for the expanded 48-team edition. With over 80,000 fans in attendance and millions watching worldwide, this halftime show is set to become one of the most talked-about moments of the entire competition. A perfect blend of music and football on the grandest night.
